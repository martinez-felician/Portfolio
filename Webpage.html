You are looking at it! After completing my Web Development certificate on Udemy, I applied that knowledge to design and build this portfolio from scratch using HTML, CSS, and Bootstrap 5.

About the Project

After completing my Web Development certificate on Udemy, I wanted to apply my new skills to a real project. I chose to build a personal portfolio — a site that could showcase all of my university projects and serve as my professional online presence.

The design process gave me freedom to explore Bootstrap's grid system, component library, and responsive utilities. I focused on making the site clean, readable, and easy to navigate — good UI/UX principles I picked up through the course.

Skills & Technologies

HTML5 & Semantic Markup

Bootstrap 5
